Privacy policy

Gradefast: OMR Sheet Scanner · Last updated 11 November 2026

Gradefast helps teachers mark multiple-choice answer sheets with a phone camera. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

The short version

  • Your exams, class lists, answer sheets and results stay on your phone. We do not have a server and we never receive them.
  • The camera is used only to read answer sheets, and the reading happens on your phone.
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it (for example an Excel file or a WhatsApp message).
  • The free app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information you enter

You may add exam names, answer keys, class names, student names, roll numbers and, optionally, a parent's phone number. This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. You can edit or delete it at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Camera and photos

When you scan, the app reads each answer sheet from the camera on your device. It keeps a straightened image of each sheet on your device so you can check unclear answers later. If you pick photos from your gallery, they are read on your device in the same way. Images are not uploaded anywhere.

Sharing that you start

When you choose to export or share results (Excel, PDF, WhatsApp, email, print, or a backup file), the app passes the file or message to the app you pick. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gradefast is free and shows a small number of ads, provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions with ads, and diagnostic information

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

Gradefast is made for teachers and other adults. It is not directed at children, and children are not asked to use it or enter information into it.

Data security and retention

Data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your phone's own security. It is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. Backups you create are files you control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
